
# Inspiria AI Growth Report
**Date:** September 25, 2026
**Priority Level:** High

### Executive Summary
Data indicates overall site growth with total sessions increasing by 10.76% and conversions rising by 19.92%, despite a severe drop in organic search visibility where impressions fell 30.86% and clicks declined 28.41%.

### 🏆 Top Wins
- Observed fact: Conversions increased by 19.92% (from 487 to 584).
- Observed fact: Total sessions grew by 10.76% (from 5,732 to 6,349).
- Observed fact: Active users increased by 8.35% (from 5,066 to 5,489).
- Observed fact: Pageviews rose by 3.48% (from 8,246 to 8,533).

### 🚨 Important Issues
- Observed fact: Google Search Console clicks fell by 28.41% (from 968 to 693).
- Observed fact: Google Search Console impressions dropped by 30.86% (from 22,573 to 15,607).

### 🔍 Possible Causes & Opportunities
- Potential contributor to conversion growth: Targeted paid campaigns, email marketing, or improved traffic quality from non-organic channels.
- Potential contributor to organic search drops: Search ranking declines, algorithmic updates, technical indexing issues, or seasonal decreases in search demand.
- Investigate specific traffic channels driving the conversion lift to reallocate marketing budget toward top-performing segments.
- Analyze query-level performance in Google Search Console to identify recoverable organic keyword positions.

### 🚀 Recommended Actions
- Investigate landing-page and query reports in Google Search Console to isolate which URLs lost the most impressions and clicks.
- Review technical SEO health, checking for recent URL changes, robots.txt blocks, or indexing errors.
- Segment GA4 conversions by source/medium to confirm which channels produced the conversion increase and evaluate scaling opportunities.
